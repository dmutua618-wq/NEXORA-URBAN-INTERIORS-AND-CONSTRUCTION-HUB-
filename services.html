Installation Process

How NEXORA Handles Every Project

Professional planning, accurate measurements, quality fabrication, and expert installation from start to finish.

Modern Kitchen Cabinet Installation Process

1. Consultation & Project Discussion
We begin by understanding your vision, preferred kitchen style, storage needs, color choices, functionality, and budget requirements.

2. Site Visit & Accurate Measurements
Our team visits the site to assess the kitchen layout, take accurate measurements, and review plumbing, electrical points, and available space.

3. Space Planning & Design Development
We carefully organize cabinet layouts, storage sections, countertop areas, and movement flow to create a functional modern kitchen.

4. 3D Kitchen Design Visualization
A detailed 3D kitchen preview is created to help you visualize cabinet arrangement, finishes, colors, and the overall final appearance.

5. Design Review, Revisions & Approval
Adjustments are made based on your feedback until the final kitchen cabinet design is fully approved.

6. Material Selection & Quotation
We help you choose suitable materials, finishes, handles, and accessories before preparing a detailed quotation.

7. Fabrication & Production
Your kitchen cabinets are professionally fabricated with precision, durability, clean finishing, and modern craftsmanship.

8. Delivery & Professional Installation
The cabinets are delivered and installed with proper alignment, secure fitting, smooth functionality, and clean finishing.

9. Final Inspection & Project Handover
We conduct a final inspection to ensure quality finishing, proper functionality, and complete client satisfaction before project handover.

Curtain Installation Process

1. Consultation & Requirement Discussion
We discuss your curtain needs, interior style, preferred fabrics, colors, functionality, and budget.

2. Site Visit & Measurements
Accurate window and room measurements are taken to ensure a perfect curtain fitting and professional finish.

3. Design Consultation & Fabric Selection
We help you select curtain fabrics, textures, styles, colors, and accessories that match your interior design.

4. 3D Visualization & Design Preview
Where necessary, we provide a visual preview showing how the curtains will appear within your room.

5. Quotation & Approval
A detailed quotation is prepared outlining materials, accessories, installation requirements, and estimated costs.

6. Tailoring & Preparation
Curtains are professionally tailored according to approved measurements and design specifications.

7. Delivery & Installation
Curtain rods, rails, hooks, and fittings are professionally mounted to ensure smooth operation and elegant presentation.

8. Final Styling & Inspection
We complete final styling, fitting checks, and inspections to ensure customer satisfaction and quality finishing.

Ceiling Mounted Bed Rail Mosquito Net Installation Process

1. Consultation & Requirement Discussion
We begin by understanding your room setup, bed size, mosquito net preferences, and installation requirements.

2. Site Visit & Measurements
Accurate room, ceiling, and bed measurements are taken to ensure proper fitting and effective coverage.

3. Design Planning & Installation Layout
We plan the rail positioning, opening direction, mounting arrangement, and overall installation structure.

4. Material Selection & Design Preview
Suitable mosquito net fabrics, rail systems, and finishing options are selected based on functionality and style.

5. Quotation & Project Approval
A clear quotation is prepared outlining materials, installation requirements, and estimated project costs.

6. Fabrication & Preparation
The mosquito net system and ceiling-mounted rails are professionally prepared according to approved specifications.

7. Delivery & Professional Installation
The ceiling rail system and mosquito net are carefully installed to ensure secure fitting, smooth operation, and full bed coverage.

8. Final Inspection & Client Handover
Final inspections are conducted to confirm proper functionality, clean finishing, durability, and customer satisfaction.
